## Intended workflow (verified in unit tests)

1. First-pass job collects evidence for frozen membership universe.
2. Immutable snapshot finalized for analyst review.
3. Analyst submits candidate set (OCC `expectedVersion`).
4. `POST /candidate-sets/technical-scan` creates a `stock_ids` scan with **only** those IDs.
5. Existing scan batches evaluate monthly then daily MACD/KD.
6. No automatic Notion report selection/publishing.
